Webmail lets you use Rootiest Mail from any browser without setting anything up. It lives at webmail.rootiest.dev and works on phones too. Your mail, calendar and contacts are all in one place there.

Signing in

  1. Go to webmail.rootiest.dev.
  2. Click Sign in with SSO.
  3. Sign in to RootiestID if it asks. You land in your inbox.

Webmail keeps you signed in on that browser, so you won't have to do this every time. To sign out, open Settings → Account and click Sign out.

App passwords are only for mail apps like Thunderbird. Webmail never asks for one.

Finding your way around

  • The bar on the far left switches between Mail, Calendar, Contacts and Files. Settings is the gear near the bottom.
  • Compose starts a new message. You can also press c, and ? shows all the keyboard shortcuts.
  • Open a message and use the buttons above it to reply, forward, archive, move or delete it.
  • Spam that gets caught lands in the Spam folder. It's worth a look now and then in case something real ended up there.

Folders

Click the + next to Your folders to make a new folder. To rename, move or delete folders, open Settings → Folders & tags. Folders are stored on the server, so they show up in your mail apps too. If a folder you expect is missing in an app, check that it's subscribed there. Each app keeps its own list of subscribed folders.

Contacts and calendars

Contacts and Calendar in the left bar use the same address books and calendars as your phone and computer, once you set up calendar and contacts syncing. A contact you add in webmail shows up on your phone and the other way around.

Filters

Filters sort your mail as it arrives, for example moving everything from a mailing list into its own folder. They run on the server, so they work even when webmail and your apps are closed.

  1. Open Settings → Filters and click New rule.
  2. Set what to look for. For example: From contains news@example.com.
  3. Under Then, pick what happens. For example: tick Move to folder and choose Newsletters.
  4. Click Save.

If you know Sieve (the language filters are written in), Edit rules as code lets you write them by hand.

Out of office replies

To answer mail automatically while you're away:

  1. Open Settings → Out of office.
  2. Write the Subject and Message people get back. If you know when you'll be away, fill in From and Until and it turns itself on and off.
  3. Turn on Reply automatically. When you're back (and didn't set an end date), turn it off again.

Your account page at mail.rootiest.dev also has Mail Rules and a Vacation Response. Please leave those alone and use webmail for both. Your mailbox runs only one set of filters at a time, and saving a mail rule or a vacation response on the account page can switch your webmail filters off without any warning. They stay off until you next save your filters or your out-of-office reply in webmail.

Out of office in webmail and Vacation Response on the account page are the same setting, so turning one on or off changes the other.

Sending from another address

If you have an alias, you can send from it:

  1. Open Settings → Account and click Edit addresses… under Email addresses.
  2. Click Add address, put the alias in Email address, and click Save. You can give it its own name and signature too.
  3. When you write a message, click From and pick the alias.

To have replies go out from whichever address the mail was sent to, turn on Reply from the address it was sent to under Settings → Composing. Masked addresses can't be used here, because they only receive.

Installing it as an app

Your browser can install webmail like an app, with its own window and icon. Click Install when webmail offers it, or use your browser's install option. On an iPhone, open it in Safari, tap the share button and pick Add to Home Screen.

Webmail can also tell you about new mail when it's closed. Click Turn on for this device when it asks, or turn it on later under Settings → Notifications. The notifications go through our own server and never include your mail.
