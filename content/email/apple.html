Apple's apps can't find the Rootiest Mail settings on their own, so you'll type in the server name once. It only takes a minute. Before you start, make an app password for the device.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Mail → Mail Accounts → Add Account. (On iOS 17 and older it's Settings → Mail → Accounts → Add Account.)
  2. Tap Other, then Add Mail Account.
  3. Fill in your Name, your Email (you@rootiest.org), your app password as the Password, and a Description like Rootiest Mail. Tap Next.
  4. Make sure IMAP is selected at the top.
  5. Under Incoming Mail Server, set Host Name to mx.rootiest.dev, User Name to your full address, and Password to your app password.
  6. Under Outgoing Mail Server, fill in exactly the same three things. It says they're optional, but they aren't.
  7. Tap Next. Your iPhone checks the account, which can take a moment.
  8. Leave Mail switched on and tap Save.

If sending doesn't work

Go back to the account in Mail Accounts, tap it, then tap SMTP under Outgoing Mail Server and tap mx.rootiest.dev. Check that Use SSL is on, Authentication is Password, and Server Port is 465.

Calendars and contacts on iPhone and iPad

These are added as separate accounts, one for each:

  1. For calendars, open Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add CalDAV Account.
  2. Set Server to mx.rootiest.dev, User Name to your full address, and Password to an app password. Tap Next, then Save.
  3. For contacts, do the same under Settings → Apps → Contacts → Contacts Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add CardDAV Account.

You can reuse the phone's mail app password for these, since it's all the same device.

Mac

  1. Open Mail and choose Mail → Add Account… from the menu bar.
  2. Pick Other Mail Account… and click Continue.
  3. Enter your Name, Email Address and your app password as the Password. Click Sign In.
  4. Mail says it can't verify the account name or password and shows more boxes. That's expected. Fill in:
    • User Name: your full address
    • Account Type: IMAP
    • Incoming Mail Server: mx.rootiest.dev
    • Outgoing Mail Server: mx.rootiest.dev
  5. Click Sign In, leave Mail ticked, and click Done.

Calendars and contacts on a Mac

  1. Open System Settings → Internet Accounts → Add Account… → Add Other Account….
  2. Choose CalDAV account for calendars, or CardDAV account for contacts.
  3. Set Account Type to Manual. Enter your full address as the User Name, your app password, and mx.rootiest.dev as the Server Address. Click Sign In.

Sending from an alias

On iPhone and iPad, open the mail account's settings, tap the Email field, and use Add Another Email to add your alias. On a Mac, open Mail → Settings → Accounts, pick the account, and choose Edit Email Addresses… from the Email Address menu.
