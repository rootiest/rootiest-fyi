Rootiest Mail

Rootiest Mail is email at your own @rootiest.org address, running on Rootiest servers. You can read it in a web browser, set it up in the mail app you already use, and keep your calendars and contacts in sync across your devices.

This guide walks you through all of it. If you're just getting started, read the first three pages in order and you'll be set.
