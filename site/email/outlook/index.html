Outlook

Outlook works with Rootiest Mail as an IMAP account. There are a few different Outlooks around, so first figure out which one you have:

  • New Outlook: the current app on Windows 11 (and the Mac). It has a simple, flat look and a Settings gear at the top right.
  • Classic Outlook: the older desktop app that comes with Microsoft 365 or Office, with a File menu at the top left.
  • Outlook for iPhone or Android.

Whichever you have, make an app password for it first.

New Outlook and the Outlook phone apps don't connect to the mail server themselves. Microsoft's servers fetch your mail and pass it on, which means your app password is stored with Microsoft. That works fine, but if you'd rather keep Microsoft out of it, Thunderbird or your device's own mail app connect directly.

New Outlook

  1. Click the Settings gear, then Accounts → Email accounts → Add account.
  2. Enter your address, you@rootiest.org, and click Continue.
  3. If Outlook asks which kind of account it is, choose IMAP.
  4. Paste your app password. If Outlook shows the server settings, check them against the list below. If it only asks for a password, it found them by itself.
  5. Click Continue and wait for it to finish.

The settings, if you need to type them in:

  • IMAP incoming server: mx.rootiest.dev, Port 993, Secure connection type SSL/TLS
  • SMTP outgoing server: mx.rootiest.dev, Port 465, Secure connection type SSL/TLS
  • Username for both: your full address. Password for both: your app password.

Classic Outlook

  1. Click File → Add Account.
  2. Enter your address and click Connect.
  3. Outlook looks up the settings. If it asks which kind of account it is, choose IMAP.
  4. Paste your app password when it asks and click Connect.
  5. Click Done.

If Outlook can't find the settings, start again, click Advanced options under the address, tick Let me set up my account manually, choose IMAP, and enter the settings from the list above. Make sure the encryption method is SSL/TLS for both servers.

Outlook for iPhone and Android

  1. Open the app's menu and tap Add account (or the settings gear, then Add mail account).
  2. Enter your address and tap Continue.
  3. If it asks for a provider, choose IMAP and turn on Advanced settings.
  4. Fill in the same settings as above, with your app password for both servers, and tap the check mark to finish.

Calendars and contacts

Outlook doesn't sync calendars or contacts from servers like this one (CalDAV and CardDAV). Only your mail shows up in Outlook. For calendars and contacts, see Calendars and contacts for apps that do.
