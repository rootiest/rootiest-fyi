Your account and signing in

Who gets an account

Rootiest Mail uses RootiestID, the one login for all the Rootiest services. If your RootiestID is in the mail group, you have a mail account. If you'd like one and don't have it yet, ask on the support desk.

Your address

Your address is your RootiestID username at rootiest.org. So if your username is sam, your address is sam@rootiest.org.

Usernames have to be all lowercase. The mail server treats addresses as lowercase, so a username with capitals in it ends up with a mailbox that can't receive anything. If your RootiestID username has any capital letters, ask on the support desk to get it fixed before you sign in to mail for the first time.

Please don't change your RootiestID username either. Your mailbox is tied to it, so a new username means a new, empty mailbox. If you really need a different name, ask on the support desk first so nothing gets lost.

Sign in once to set it up

Your mailbox is created the first time you sign in, so do that before you give your address to anyone. Mail sent to you before then bounces back to the sender. The easiest way is to open webmail and sign in.

How signing in works

Your mail account doesn't have a password of its own. On the web you always sign in with RootiestID, the same way you sign in to everything else:

  • Webmail at webmail.rootiest.dev: click Sign in with SSO.
  • Your account page at mail.rootiest.dev/account: click Sign in with RootiestID. This is where you make app passwords and change a few account settings.

If you're already signed in to RootiestID in that browser, you go straight in. Otherwise RootiestID asks you to sign in first, the usual way.

Mail apps on your computer or phone (Thunderbird, Apple Mail, Outlook and the rest) can't do the RootiestID sign-in, so they use an app password instead. That page explains how to make one.

Signing out

Signing out of webmail only ends your webmail session. Your mail apps keep working, because they use their own app passwords. To cut off an app (say you lost your phone), delete its app password. See removing an app password.
