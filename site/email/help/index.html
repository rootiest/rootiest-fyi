Getting help

Check these first

  • Password errors in an app? Mail apps need an app password, and the username is your full address. Making a fresh app password fixes most of these.
  • Can't connect at all? Compare your app's settings with the manual settings. The server is mx.rootiest.dev, and both ports use SSL/TLS.
  • Missing a folder? Check it's subscribed in that app (see Folders).
  • Mail from someone hasn't arrived? Look in your Junk folder.

Ask on the support desk

If that doesn't sort it out, open a ticket at support.rootiest.dev. Sign in with your RootiestID and click Open a new ticket. Pick Mail or Webmail as the service, and Account & Sign-in or Report a Problem as the category.

If you can't sign in to RootiestID at all, the support desk has a link to contact us by email instead.

What to include

The more of this you include, the quicker it gets fixed:

  • Your Rootiest address.
  • Which app and device you're using, like Thunderbird on Windows or Mail on iPhone, or webmail.
  • What you were trying to do, and what happened instead.
  • The exact error message, word for word. A screenshot is great.
  • About when it happened, and whether it still does.
  • For mail that went missing: who sent it (or who you sent it to), and roughly when.

Never include an app password or anything else secret, and cover them up in screenshots. Nobody needs your passwords to help you.
