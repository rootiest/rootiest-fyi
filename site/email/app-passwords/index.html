App passwords

Your mail account has no ordinary password. You sign in on the web with RootiestID, which mail apps can't do. So each app gets an app password instead: a long random password the server makes for you, used by one app and nothing else.

That has a nice side effect. Every device gets its own app password, so if you lose your phone you delete the phone's app password and everything else keeps working.

When you need one

  • Any mail app: Thunderbird, Apple Mail, Outlook, phone mail apps and so on.
  • Calendar and contacts syncing (CalDAV and CardDAV).

You don't need one for webmail. Webmail uses RootiestID.

Create an app password

You make app passwords in webmail.

  1. Go to webmail.rootiest.dev and sign in.
  2. Open Settings → Password & sign-in and scroll down to App passwords.
  3. Click New app password.
  4. In App name, write what it's for, like Thunderbird on my laptop or iPhone. This is just so you can tell them apart later.
  5. Click Create. You can leave More options closed.
  6. Your new app password shows up in a box. Copy it (the button next to it does that) and paste it into your mail app right away. Then click Done.

The app password is only shown once. If you lose it before you've put it in your app, that's fine: delete that app password and make a new one.

Tips

  • Make a separate app password for each device or app. It costs nothing and makes cleaning up easy.
  • Your mail app saves the app password, so you don't need to keep a copy anywhere else.
  • Never send an app password to anyone, including the support desk. Nobody will ever ask for it.

Remove an app password

Do this when you stop using a device, sell or lose it, or think an app password got out.

  1. In webmail, open Settings → Password & sign-in and scroll down to App passwords.
  2. Find the app password by its name and click the bin icon next to it.
  3. Click Delete when it asks Delete app password?

It stops working right away. The app that used it will start asking for a password, and every other app keeps going as normal.

If your app says the password is wrong

  • Check the username is your full address (you@rootiest.org), not just your name.
  • Check you pasted the app password, not your RootiestID details. RootiestID can't be used in a mail app.
  • Make sure nothing extra got pasted along with it, like a space at the end.
  • Still stuck? Make a fresh app password and try that one.
