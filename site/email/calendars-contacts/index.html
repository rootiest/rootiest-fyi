Calendars and contacts

Your Rootiest Mail account comes with a calendar and an address book on the server. Connect them to your devices and anything you add on one shows up on the others.

Apps connect to them with two standards: CalDAV for calendars and CardDAV for contacts. Like mail apps, they sign in with your full address and an app password.

WhatAddress
Calendars (CalDAV)https://mx.rootiest.dev/dav/cal/
Contacts (CardDAV)https://mx.rootiest.dev/dav/card/
Server name, for apps that only ask for thatmx.rootiest.dev

Android with DAVx⁵

Android doesn't do CalDAV or CardDAV by itself. DAVx⁵ fills that gap: it syncs your Rootiest calendars and contacts into the phone's own Calendar and Contacts apps, so they work with whatever calendar app you already use. It's free on F-Droid and costs a few dollars on the Play Store.

  1. Make an app password for it (or reuse your phone's mail app password).
  2. Open DAVx⁵ and tap + to add an account.
  3. Choose Login with URL and user name.
  4. Set Base URL to https://mx.rootiest.dev/, User name to your full address, and Password to the app password. Tap Login.
  5. DAVx⁵ finds your calendars and address books. Tap Create account.
  6. On the CardDAV and CalDAV tabs, tick the address books and calendars you want to sync.

Give Android's battery saving a pass for DAVx⁵ if it asks, otherwise it may not sync in the background.

Other apps

Most calendar and contacts apps with a CalDAV or CardDAV option will work. Give them the addresses in the table at the top, your full address as the username, and an app password. If an app only asks for a server, use mx.rootiest.dev.
